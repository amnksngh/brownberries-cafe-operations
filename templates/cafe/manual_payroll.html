Rules and workflow

Confirm employment → review each calendar day → confirm that month's salary → finalize → record actual payment. Finalized months are locked. Nothing is paid automatically.

P = Present; PL = Paid Leave; UL = Unpaid Leave; FH/SH = First/Second Half Present; BL = Bereavement; SL = Sick Leave. PL requires two days' notice; SL requires notification by the day of absence. FH/SH earns half-day pay and the other half is paid only with a timely half-day application and remaining allowance.

PL and SL share an allowance: full calendar month employed = 2 days; partial month with 15 or more calendar days = 1; fewer than 15 = 0. Eligible BL has a separate five-day January–December cap. Excess leave is unpaid. Historical cash entitlement cannot pay for later leave.

Unused allowance is valued at that month's confirmed salary ÷ calendar days and paid with March payroll or eligible exit payroll. Exit requires 15 days of notice served or management release; otherwise the unused cash balance is forfeited. Opening resets are audited per staff account, including admins when explicitly included at cutover.
