Prepared Items, SOPs & Responsibilities Experimental

Recipe defines what goes into an item; SOP defines how it is made; the responsibility plan defines who should perform it. Approved versions remain immutable.

This is part of Menu Management. It contains menu-linked products and internally prepared components such as sauces or dough. Raw stock such as cabbage, milk, and packaging stays in Inventory Management, although it can still be selected as a recipe input. Customer visibility remains controlled only by the Menu Items screen.
How to create a versioned SOP
  1. Select a prepared or sellable item from the left. For a sauce, dough, or other prepared component, create the internal item first.
  2. Review its Overview and set the production route and default workstation.
  3. In Recipe, start a draft, add inventory ingredients or prepared components, then approve the recipe when it is correct.
  4. In Standard Operating Procedure, click Start Draft SOP, complete the header, and save it.
  5. Add each step in working order. Include active/passive time, workstation, role, skill, and quality checkpoint where relevant.
  6. In Responsibility Matrix, assign a primary employee and any backups to every required role.
  7. Review Contribution and Validation. Resolve errors before approval.
  8. Click Approve & Lock SOP + Responsibility Plan. Use Create New Draft SOP Revision for future changes; the approved history is retained.
