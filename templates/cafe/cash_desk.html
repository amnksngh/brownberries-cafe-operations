What does the ledger / denomination gap mean?

This is the cash ledger balance minus the value of recorded notes and coins. Older cash payments recorded without denominations can leave a gap. It is not confirmed extra cash, today's earnings, or automatically a loss. Use a physical count and review the original payments before correcting anything.
